# Evolution of Voice AI

- **1980s-1990s:** Formant & Concatenative Synthesis (Stiff, robotic).
- **2016-2018:** Deep Learning TTS (WaveNet, Tacotron 2). High quality, but slow and disconnected from reasoning.
- **2023:** Early Voice Agents. Brittle, high-latency chains of ASR -> ChatGPT -> TTS APIs.
- **Present (Auralis Platform):** Unified Voice Intelligence. Hardware-aware dynamic TTS engines running concurrently alongside lightning-fast Groq LPU reasoning and localized RAG knowledge bases.
