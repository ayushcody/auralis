# The Limitations of Traditional Voice AI

- **Robotic Delivery:** Legacy TTS systems lack emotional depth and contextual prosody.
- **Data Heavy & Siloed:** Traditional voice cloning requires hours of studio recording. Building conversational AI requires stitching together disparate, incompatible tools.
- **High Latency & Hardware Limits:** Running ASR, LLMs, and TTS concurrently on local hardware leads to massive GPU VRAM bottlenecks and unresponsive UX.
